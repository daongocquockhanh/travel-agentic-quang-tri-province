## Quick anchors

- **Atmosphere:** quiet dignity. Trustworthy local guide. Three audience tracks share one product (war / foreign / domestic) — track tints small accents only, never repaint the UI.
- **Primary color** `#0F4C5C` · **Accent** `#F2A73A` · **Paper** `#F7F4EE` · **Ink** `#1F2428`.
- **Type:** Newsreader (display) · Be Vietnam Pro (body) · JetBrains Mono (citations).
- **Vietnamese diacritics are sacred** — never strip. Test every layout against `Việt Nam · Quảng Trị · Vĩnh Mốc · Hội An · Đông Hà`.
- **No emoji, no exclamation marks in war-track copy, no gradients, no spring animations, no carnival color.** See VISUAL FOUNDATIONS in the README.
