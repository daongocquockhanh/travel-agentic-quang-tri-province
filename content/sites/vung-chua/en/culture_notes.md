This is a grave, and visitors treat it as one. Dress modestly, speak quietly, and follow the guards' guidance on where to stand and how to offer incense; incense is lit at the grave in turn. Avoid photographing other people while they pay their respects.

The busiest days are national holidays and 22 December, the founding day of the Vietnam People's Army, when thousands come and queues form.
