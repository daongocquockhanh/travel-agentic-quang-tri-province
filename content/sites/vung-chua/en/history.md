Võ Nguyên Giáp was born on 25 August 1911 in Lộc Thủy, in the former Lệ Thủy district of what was then Quảng Bình province. He became the first general of the Vietnam People's Army and commanded the Điện Biên Phủ campaign of 1954.

He died in Hà Nội on 4 October 2013, at the age of 102. After a state funeral on 12–13 October 2013, he was buried at Vũng Chùa – Đảo Yến on the afternoon of 13 October. The place was chosen in keeping with his own wishes and his family's: a return to the province where he was born.

Since then the grave has received a steady stream of visitors, especially on national holidays.
