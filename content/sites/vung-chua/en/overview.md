Vũng Chùa – Đảo Yến is a quiet stretch of coast in the north of the province where General Võ Nguyên Giáp is buried. His grave sits on Thọ mountain at Rồng cape, looking out to sea towards the small island of Đảo Yến (Swallow Island).

General Giáp (1911–2013) was the first general of the Vietnam People's Army and commanded the Điện Biên Phủ campaign of 1954. For many Vietnamese the grave is a place of pilgrimage, and families and delegations come from across the country to light incense.
