The site is a little over two kilometres east of National Route 1, near the Ngang Pass in the far north of the province, and roughly an hour's drive north of Đồng Hới. Entry is free.

A walk and some steps lead up the hill to the grave; allow about an hour. Mornings are calmer than holiday afternoons, and the shore below has a clear view of Đảo Yến.
