The riverside by Quảng Trị town is a short drive from Đông Hà. Combine a walk on the bank and the wharf with the Quảng Trị Citadel memorial park next to it, and with La Vang a few kilometres away; together they make a calm half-day.

Around 27 July evenings at the wharf are crowded and moving; on other days the river is quiet. Bring insect repellent for dusk.
