Releasing flowers and floating candles on the Thạch Hãn is a living memorial custom. It is often traced to April 1987, when a veteran of the 1972 battle bought all the flowers at the town market and let them float down the river for his fallen comrades.

Today ceremonies take place on major anniversaries, above all around 27 July, War Invalids and Martyrs' Day, when candles are released at dusk from the riverside wharf near the citadel. Visitors may join quietly; flowers and candles are sold nearby on those days.
