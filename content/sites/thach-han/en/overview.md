The Thạch Hãn is the main river of central Quảng Trị. It flows past Quảng Trị town, where the old citadel stands on its bank, and reaches the sea at Cửa Việt; its lower course is also called the Cửa Việt river.

For many Vietnamese the river is above all a place of remembrance: it is closely tied to the 81-day battle for the Quảng Trị Citadel in 1972, and people release flowers and floating candles on it in memory of those who died. On quiet days it is also simply a broad, green river, good for an early-morning or sunset walk along the bank.
