Allow a full day. Phong Nha Cave is reached by boat from the wharf in Phong Nha town; boats are shared by small groups, and the trip combines the river and the cave. Paradise Cave is a separate drive, with a long wooden walkway inside and a climb of steps at the entrance.

Wear shoes with grip: cave paths can be wet and slippery. Bring water and a light layer, because the caves stay cool. Sơn Đoòng and the other wild caves can only be visited on licensed multi-day expeditions, booked well in advance, with limited places each season.

The autumn rains in central Vietnam (roughly September to December) can close the boat trips and some caves when the rivers run high, so check before you go.
