Đông Hà is the main town of central Quảng Trị and the most practical base for exploring the DMZ sites. It stands where National Route 1 crosses the start of Route 9, the road west to the Lao border at Lao Bảo.

Đông Hà was the provincial capital until 1 July 2025, when Quảng Trị merged with Quảng Bình and the provincial centre moved to Đồng Hới. It remains the hub for hotels, buses, markets and food in this part of the province.
