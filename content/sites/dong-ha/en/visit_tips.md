Most DMZ day trips start from Đông Hà: Vĩnh Mốc and Hiền Lương to the north, Trường Sơn and Khe Sanh to the west, La Vang and the Thạch Hãn to the south, and Cửa Việt for the boat to Cồn Cỏ. The town is on the north–south railway and Highway 1.

Book a car with driver or join a tour for the western sites; distances there are long. Evenings along the Hiếu River are pleasant for a walk and street food.
