Đông Hà is a good place to eat your way through Quảng Trị cooking. Look for cháo bột (thick rice-noodle soup, often with fish or eel), bánh ướt cuốn thịt nướng (soft rice paper rolled with grilled pork and herbs), and small steamed cakes such as bánh lọc, bánh nậm and bánh bèo. Mai Xá mussel noodles (bún hến), made with mussels from the nearby Hiếu River, are a regional favourite.

Đông Hà market in the town centre is the place for dried seafood, spices and local specialities to take home.
