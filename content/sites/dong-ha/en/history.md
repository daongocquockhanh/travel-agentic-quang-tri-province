Đông Hà grew up at a river crossing and road junction, and that position shaped its history. During the war a large US combat base stood here; the town has since grown over it, and the old runway is now one of the main streets.

After 1975 Đông Hà developed into the provincial capital of Quảng Trị. Route 9, which starts here, is today part of the East–West Economic Corridor linking central Vietnam with Laos and Thailand.
