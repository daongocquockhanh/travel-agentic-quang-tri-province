During the war Cồn Cỏ was a strategic outpost off the coast near the 17th parallel and came under repeated attack. Its defenders were given the title "Hero Island", and the island is remembered in Vietnam as a symbol of steadfastness.

Wartime sites on the island include military tunnels, among them a medical tunnel, and a traditional exhibition room. In 2025, under the national administrative reform, Cồn Cỏ became a special administrative zone of the merged Quảng Trị province.
