Cồn Cỏ is a small island of about 2.3 km², roughly 28–30 km offshore from Cửa Việt. Much of it is still covered by native tropical forest, ringed by black basalt rocks, clear water and small beaches such as Bến Nghè.

The island is now a special administrative zone of Quảng Trị, home to a small community and a military garrison. Visitors come for the forest, the snorkelling, the lighthouse view and the island's wartime sites.
