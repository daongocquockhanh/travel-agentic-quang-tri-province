Boats leave from Cửa Việt; the crossing takes roughly an hour to an hour and a half depending on the boat and the sea. Departures are weather-dependent and not daily in every season, so confirm the schedule a day ahead and keep your plans flexible. The calmest months are usually late spring and summer.

A day trip gives about four hours on the island. Bring cash, sunscreen, water and something for seasickness. The island is small enough to walk; bicycles and snorkelling gear are often available to hire. Some areas are military; follow signs and instructions.
