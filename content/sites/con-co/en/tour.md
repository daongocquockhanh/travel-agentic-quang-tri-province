## A small island far offshore

> Stepping off the boat

You have crossed roughly twenty-eight to thirty kilometres of sea from Cửa Việt to reach Cồn Cỏ, a small island of about 2.3 square kilometres. Much of it is still covered by native tropical forest, ringed by black basalt rocks, clear water and small beaches such as Bến Nghè.

The island is small enough to walk. A day trip gives you about four hours here, so plan the time for the forest, the lighthouse view and the island's wartime sites.

## Hero Island

> At the traditional exhibition room

During the war, Cồn Cỏ was a strategic outpost off the coast near the 17th parallel, and it came under repeated attack. Its defenders were given the title "Hero Island", and the island is remembered in Vietnam as a symbol of steadfastness.

## The tunnels

> By the military tunnels

The island's wartime sites include military tunnels, among them a medical tunnel, and the traditional exhibition room.

Some areas of the island are military. Please follow the signs and the instructions you are given.

## The island today

> Back by the shore

In 2025, under the national administrative reform, Cồn Cỏ became a special administrative zone of the merged Quảng Trị province. It is home to a small community and a military garrison.

Visitors come for the forest and the snorkelling; bicycles and snorkelling gear are often available to hire. Keep an eye on the time: the boat back depends on the weather.
