The river mouth has long been the sea gateway to central Quảng Trị. During the war, a US Marine logistics base was established at Cửa Việt in 1966; supplies moved up the river to Đông Hà and on to bases along the demilitarised zone, and the area saw heavy fighting in 1968.

Today the port is being developed for fishing and for tourism, including the Cửa Việt – Cồn Cỏ passenger service.
