Cửa Việt is about 15 minutes' drive from Đông Hà. Early morning, when boats unload at the fish market, is the liveliest time. The beach is best from late spring to late summer; avoid swimming near the river mouth and the harbour channel.

For Cồn Cỏ, check the boat schedule a day ahead: departures depend on the weather and are not daily in every season.
