Cửa Việt is where the Thạch Hãn river meets the sea, northeast of Đông Hà. It is a working fishing port and a long sandy beach, popular with families from Đông Hà on summer weekends.

It is also the departure point for boats to Cồn Cỏ island. If you plan to visit the island, you will start here.
