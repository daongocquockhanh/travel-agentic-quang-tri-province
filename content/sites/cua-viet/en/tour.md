## A working river mouth

> At the harbour

This is Cửa Việt, where the Thạch Hãn river meets the sea, northeast of Đông Hà. It is a working fishing port, and early morning, when the boats unload at the fish market, is the liveliest time to be here.

Behind you runs a long sandy beach, popular with families from Đông Hà on summer weekends.

## The sea gateway

> Looking up the river

This river mouth has long been the sea gateway to central Quảng Trị. During the war, a US Marine logistics base was set up here in 1966. Supplies moved up the river to Đông Hà, and on to the bases along the demilitarised zone. The area saw heavy fighting in 1968.

## The boat to Cồn Cỏ

> By the passenger pier

Today the port is being developed for fishing and for tourism, including the passenger service to Cồn Cỏ island. If you plan to visit the island, you start here.

Check the boat schedule a day ahead: departures depend on the weather and are not daily in every season. If you swim, stay away from the river mouth and the harbour channel.
