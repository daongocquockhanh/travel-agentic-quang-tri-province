Khe Sanh is well inland, roughly an hour and a half by road from Đông Hà along Route 9, so plan it as a half-day or combine it with other stops in the west of the province. Allow about 75 minutes on site: the museum is small, and most of the time is spent walking the grounds and the airstrip.

The plateau is higher and can be cooler and wetter than the coast, so bring a light layer in the rainy season. Many visitors are veterans or their families; please photograph thoughtfully and keep voices low.
