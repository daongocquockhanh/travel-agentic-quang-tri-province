Khe Sanh lies on a plateau in the hills of western Quảng Trị, just south of Route 9 on the road to the Lao border. The former combat base here was the scene of one of the longest and best-known battles of the Vietnam War, in 1968.

Today the site is a museum and open-air display. A small museum holds photographs and objects, and outside are aircraft (including a C-130 and a CH-47 Chinook), artillery, restored bunkers and parts of the old airstrip. It is a place of memory for veterans and families on all sides.
