Đồng Hới is a coastal city at the mouth of the Nhật Lệ River and, since 1 July 2025, the centre of the merged Quảng Trị province. It is the main base for the Phong Nha caves to the north-west, with an airport, a station on the north–south railway and Nhật Lệ beach on its doorstep.

Its landmarks are small and close together: the brick gate of Quảng Bình Quan, what remains of the Đồng Hới Citadel, and the bell tower of Tam Tòa Church, left standing in ruins as a memorial.
