The three landmarks are within a few kilometres of each other and can be seen in a morning by bicycle or taxi. Tam Tòa's bell tower stands near the Nhật Lệ River; it is a memorial, so visit quietly.

Nhật Lệ beach is at its best early or late in the day, when the sun is lower. Đồng Hới also makes a practical overnight stop before or after the Phong Nha caves.
