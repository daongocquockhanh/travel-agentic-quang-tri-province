In the seventeenth century this area lay on the frontier between the Trịnh and Nguyễn lords. In 1630 the strategist Đào Duy Từ began the Lũy Thầy defensive walls, and in 1631 the Quảng Bình Quan gate was built on them. The gate was almost destroyed by bombing during the war; what stands today is a restoration.

The Đồng Hới Citadel was begun in 1812 under Emperor Gia Long, first in earth, and rebuilt in brick in 1824 under Emperor Minh Mạng. Parts of its walls and gates survive in the city centre.

Tam Tòa Church was built in 1887. It was destroyed by American bombing on 11 February 1965, leaving only the bell tower. In 1997 the province listed the bell tower area as a relic to be preserved.
