Allow about an hour. Paths between the zones are long and partly unshaded, so mornings are best and water is worth carrying. Incense and flowers are usually sold near the gate.

From Đông Hà the drive is about 35 to 45 minutes. Trường Sơn pairs naturally with Khe Sanh or with the Route 9 corridor on a day in the west of the province.
