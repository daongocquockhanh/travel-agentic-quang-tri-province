Visitors usually light incense at the central memorial before walking among the graves, and many leave flowers or incense at individual headstones, especially those marked as unidentified. Dress modestly, with shoulders and knees covered, speak quietly, and do not step on or sit against graves.

The busiest time is around 27 July, Vietnam's War Invalids and Martyrs' Day, when many families and delegations visit; expect crowds and ceremonies then. Ask before photographing people who are mourning.
