Trường Sơn National Martyrs' Cemetery lies on low hills beside Highway 15, about 28 km northwest of Đông Hà. It is the largest martyrs' cemetery in Vietnam, the resting place of more than 10,000 People's Army of Vietnam soldiers and volunteers who died along the Trường Sơn route — known in the West as the Ho Chi Minh Trail — and around the 17th parallel.

The graves are arranged in zones by the home provinces of the fallen, around a central memorial on a hilltop. It is a place of mourning first: families travel from across the country to visit relatives here.
