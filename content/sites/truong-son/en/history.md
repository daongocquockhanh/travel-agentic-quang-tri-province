Construction began on 24 October 1975, a few months after the war ended, and was completed on 10 April 1977. The site was chosen in the Bến Tắt area, beside Highway 15, which had been part of the wartime Trường Sơn route.

The cemetery holds more than 10,000 graves. They are grouped into regional zones named after the soldiers' home provinces, and there is a section for soldiers who could not be identified. The memorial area stands at the centre of the hill.
