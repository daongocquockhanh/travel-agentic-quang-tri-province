Walk the bridge in both directions: the change of colour at the midpoint is the clearest way to feel what the demarcation line meant. Allow about 45 minutes for the bridge, the north bank flagpole and the exhibition on the south bank. The site is open-air with little shade, so mornings and late afternoons are more comfortable, and a hat and water help in summer.

Hiền Lương sits between Vĩnh Mốc Tunnels and Cửa Tùng beach, so the three combine easily into one half-day along the old 17th-parallel line. Please keep voices low near the monuments; many visitors come here to remember family members.
