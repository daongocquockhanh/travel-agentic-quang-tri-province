Hiền Lương Bridge crosses the Bến Hải River close to the 17th parallel. Under the 1954 Geneva Accords the river became the temporary military demarcation line between North and South Vietnam, and the bridge became the best-known symbol of a divided country for the next two decades.

Today the relic complex spans both banks. On the north bank stand the tall flagpole and the old border police post; on the south bank, an exhibition house and a monument group on the theme of the wish for reunification. The site was listed as a national relic in 1986 and as a Special National Relic in 2013.
