In the early twentieth century Cửa Tùng was one of the best-known resorts in central Vietnam. The French called it the "queen of beaches", and villas and guesthouses stood on the hills above the sea.

The estuary lies on the Bến Hải, which from 1954 formed the temporary demarcation line between North and South, and the area was heavily affected by the war. Today it is mainly a local beach and fishing area, busiest in summer.
