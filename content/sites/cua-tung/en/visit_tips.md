The best swimming months are roughly May to August; from autumn into winter the sea is often rough, and storms can close the beach. Swim only where locals do, and stay out of the water near the river mouth, where currents are stronger.

Seafood restaurants line the beach road; ask for prices before ordering. Combine Cửa Tùng with Hiền Lương and Vĩnh Mốc for a full day along the old demarcation line.
