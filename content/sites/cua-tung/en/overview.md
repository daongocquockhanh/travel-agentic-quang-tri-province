Cửa Tùng is a long, gently sloping beach of fine sand at the mouth of the Bến Hải River, in the former Vĩnh Linh district. Low red basalt headlands run out into the sea along this stretch of coast, with casuarina trees behind the sand.

It is one of the most popular beaches in Quảng Trị and sits only a few kilometres from both Hiền Lương Bridge and the Vĩnh Mốc Tunnels, so it is an easy place to rest after a morning on the 17th-parallel sites.
