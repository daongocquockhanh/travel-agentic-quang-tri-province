## The queen of beaches

> On the sand, facing the sea

You are on Cửa Tùng, a long, gently sloping beach of fine sand at the mouth of the Bến Hải River, in the former Vĩnh Linh district. Look along the coast: low red basalt headlands run out into the sea, and casuarina trees stand behind the sand.

In the early twentieth century this was one of the best-known resorts in central Vietnam. The French called it the "queen of beaches", and villas and guesthouses stood on the hills above the water.

## Where the dividing line met the sea

> Looking towards the river mouth

The estuary beside you is the mouth of the Bến Hải, the river that from 1954 formed the temporary demarcation line between North and South. Hiền Lương Bridge and the Vĩnh Mốc Tunnels are only a few kilometres away, and the area was heavily affected by the war.

Today Cửa Tùng is mainly a local beach and fishing area, busiest in summer.

## Before you swim

> Back on the beach

The best months for swimming are roughly May to August. From autumn into winter the sea is often rough, and storms can close the beach.

Swim only where locals do, and stay out of the water near the river mouth, where the currents are stronger. Seafood restaurants line the beach road; ask for prices before you order.
