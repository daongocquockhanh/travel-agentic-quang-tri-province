The site sits on a low coastal bluff above a beach; combine a tunnel visit with a short walk to the East Sea entrances for context. Wear flat shoes — the floor is uneven and sometimes slippery. The ceilings are low; tall visitors should crouch through the deeper passages. A guide is included with the ticket and is strongly recommended; English-speaking guides are usually available but not guaranteed in advance. Bring a small torch even though the system is lit. The site museum at the entrance is small but worth ten to fifteen minutes for context maps and photographs. Allow about ninety minutes total. Mornings are cooler and quieter than afternoons.
