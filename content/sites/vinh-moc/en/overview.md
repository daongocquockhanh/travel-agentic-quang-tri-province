The Vinh Moc Tunnels are an underground village in the former Vinh Linh district, on the coast of Quang Tri Province, about 30 km north of Dong Ha. The tunnel system was hand-dug by villagers between 1965 and 1967 so that the entire community of Vinh Moc could continue to live, study, and work below ground while the surface was repeatedly bombed during the Vietnam War. The complex sits at three depths reaching roughly 23 metres below the surface and includes wells, a meeting room, kitchens, a small clinic, and family chambers. Today it operates as a national historic site with guided walking tours.
