La Vang, about 5 km southwest of Quảng Trị town, is the most important Catholic pilgrimage site in Vietnam. The shrine honours Our Lady of La Vang and commemorates a Marian apparition that, according to Catholic tradition, took place here in 1798.

Pope John XXIII raised the church to the rank of minor basilica in 1961. The basilica built in the 1920s was destroyed in 1972; its bell tower survives. A large new basilica, in a traditional Vietnamese style and designed to hold about 5,000 people, has been under construction since 2012. Pilgrims of all backgrounds are welcome, and many non-Catholic visitors come for the calm grounds.
