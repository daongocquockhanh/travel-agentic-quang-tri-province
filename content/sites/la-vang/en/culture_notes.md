La Vang is an active place of worship. Dress modestly (shoulders and knees covered), speak quietly, and avoid photographing people at prayer or during Mass without permission. Visitors who are not Catholic are welcome to walk the grounds and see the old bell tower.

The busiest times are the Marian feasts, especially around the Assumption on 15 August, when tens of thousands of pilgrims gather; accommodation and roads nearby fill up then.
