La Vang is a short drive from Quảng Trị town and about 20 to 25 minutes from Đông Hà. Allow about an hour to see the old bell tower, the outdoor statues and the grounds. The site is open early in the morning until the evening, which makes it a good stop when other sites have closed.

Avoid arriving in the middle of a Mass if you only want to look around; schedules are posted at the shrine.
